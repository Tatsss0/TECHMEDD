📧 Email Setup Guide

Get your TechMed email notifications working in 5 minutes!

🚨 The Problem

XAMPP's PHP mail() function doesn't work by default!
You need to configure SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) to send emails.

✅ The Solution: Gmail SMTP

1

Get Gmail App Password

  1. Go to your Google Account: myaccount.google.com
  2. Enable 2-Factor Authentication (if not already enabled):
    • Security → 2-Step Verification
    • Follow the prompts
  3. Generate App Password:
    • Visit: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
    • Select app: "Mail"
    • Select device: "Windows Computer"
    • Click "Generate"
  4. SAVE the 16-character password!
Example App Password: abcd efgh ijkl mnop
(You'll need this in Step 2)
2

Configure SMTP Settings

  1. Open this file: c:\xampp\htdocs\config\smtp-config.php
  2. Find these lines and replace with YOUR values:
define('SMTP_USER', 'ourtechmedcompany@gmail.com'); // Your Gmail define('SMTP_PASS', 'your-app-password-here'); // App Password (NO SPACES!) define('MAIL_FROM', 'ourtechmedcompany@gmail.com'); // Your Gmail
⚠️ Remove all spaces from the App Password!
Example: If password is "abcd efgh ijkl mnop"
Enter as: "abcdefghijklmnop"

Example Configuration:

define('SMTP_USER', 'john.doe@gmail.com'); define('SMTP_PASS', 'abcdefghijklmnop'); define('MAIL_FROM', 'john.doe@gmail.com');
3

Test Your Configuration

🧪 Open Test Page
  1. The test page should now show:
    Configuration:
    • SMTP Host: smtp.gmail.com ✅
    • SMTP User: your.email@gmail.com ✅
    • SMTP Port: 587 ✅
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Click "Send Test Email"
  4. Check your inbox (and spam folder!)
4

Test Reschedule Notification

  1. Log into your TechMed patient account
  2. Reschedule any appointment
  3. Press F12 to open browser console
  4. Look for: ✅ Reschedule notification email sent successfully
  5. Check your email!

✅ Verification Checklist

  • Test page shows "SMTP Host: smtp.gmail.com" (NOT "NOT CONFIGURED")
  • Test email arrives in your inbox
  • Reschedule notification arrives after rescheduling
  • Browser console shows "✅ Email sent successfully"
  • No errors in console (F12)

❌ Common Problems & Solutions

Error: "Could not instantiate mail function"
Solution: Configure SMTP (follow steps above). PHP mail() doesn't work on XAMPP without config.
Error: "SMTP connect() failed"
Solution: Check your App Password. Make sure you're using the App Password, NOT your regular Gmail password. Remove all spaces from the password.
Error: "Authentication failed"
Solution:
  • Verify 2FA is enabled on Google account
  • Generate a NEW App Password
  • Copy it without spaces
  • Update smtp-config.php
Console shows "✅ Email sent" but no email received?
Solution:
  • Check spam/junk folder
  • Wait a few minutes (sometimes delayed)
  • Check PHP error logs: C:\xampp\php\logs\php_error_log
  • Verify patient account has correct email address in Firestore
